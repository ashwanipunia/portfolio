Academic Qualifications & Education

Formal university education and scientific research training in biological sciences, agricultural biotechnology, and molecular biochemistry from premier institutes in India.

2020 – 2025

Ph.D. in Biological Sciences

Academy of Scientific & Innovative Research (AcSIR)

Research Laboratory: CSIR-Institute of Himalayan Bioresource Technology (CSIR-IHBT), Palampur, HP, India

Doctoral dissertation centered on high-resolution mass spectrometry-driven metabolomics, proteomics, and phytochemical characterization of endangered and economically vital Himalayan medicinal plants.

  • Specialized focus in UHPLC-QTOF-IMS method development, untargeted and targeted metabolomics, and DIA/DDA proteomics.
  • Comprehensive research resulting in 8 high-impact international publications and book chapters.
  • Conferred under the aegis of AcSIR, an Institution of National Importance established by an Act of Parliament.
2018 – 2020

M.Tech in Agricultural Biotechnology

Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IIT Kharagpur)

West Bengal, India

Advanced technical education at India's first Indian Institute of Technology, focusing on modern biotechnological applications in agriculture, bioprocessing, and molecular biochemistry.

  • Rigorous coursework in bioprocess engineering, molecular biology, genomics, and analytical bio-instrumentation.
  • Hands-on research methodologies, laboratory analytics, and statistical experimental design.
2016 – 2018

M.Sc in Biotechnology

Central University of Haryana

Jant-Pali, Mahendergarh, Haryana, India

Core postgraduate education in fundamental and applied biotechnology, biochemistry, and molecular biology.

  • Comprehensive training in enzymology, microbiology, molecular genetics, and plant tissue culture.
  • Foundation leading to early qualification in national fellowship examinations (Joint CSIR-UGC NET 2017 & GATE 2018).

National Competitive Standing

Throughout his postgraduate and doctoral studies, Dr. Ashwani Punia consistently secured national qualifications in both the Joint CSIR-UGC National Eligibility Test (NET) (2017, 2018, 2019) and the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) in Biotechnology (2018, 2019, 2020, 2022), attesting to continuous academic rigor and foundational scientific excellence.
