About Me

As a dedicated and resourceful biochemist, I possess over five years of rigorous research experience specializing in mass spectrometry-driven metabolomics, proteomics, and lipidomics. My research operates at the intersection of analytical chemistry and systems biology, utilizing advanced high-resolution instruments coupled with robust chemometric and bioinformatics workflows.

I am highly committed to developing innovative extraction, isolation, and separation techniques for plant and mammalian biomolecules. Through multi-omics integration and structural analysis, my goal is to decode complex regulatory pathways, investigate stress adaptation mechanisms in medicinal plants, and uncover therapeutic bioactive molecules.

Research Interests

Plant Metabolomics

Deciphering the complexity of plant specialized metabolism, constructing comprehensive metabolite reference maps, and exploring how light regimes and environmental cues regulate secondary biosynthesis pathways in medicinal flora.

Mass Spectrometry Imaging

Spatially mapping metabolites and proteins directly within tissue sections to understand their localized distribution, biosynthesis sites, and transport mechanics under physiological and developmental shifts.

Proteomics in Human/Plant Systems

Using DIA/DDA pipelines, medicinal proteomics, and intact protein/PTM analysis to discover regulatory networks governing cardiac disease models, developmental transitions, and plant environmental adaptations.

Bioactive Peptide Discovery

Identifying, isolating, and validating novel plant-derived bioactive peptides for pharmaceutical applications, assessing their therapeutic potential in cardio-protection and metabolic diseases.

Technical Competencies

Mass Spectrometry

Instrumentation Expertise
