Research Profile & Mission

Operating at the convergence of high-resolution analytical chemistry, computational biology, and systems metabolomics.

Analytical Chemistry & Systems Biology

As a biochemist and mass spectrometry specialist, I possess over five years of research experience focused on mass spectrometry-driven metabolomics, proteomics, and lipidomics. My scientific work bridges analytical instrumentation and molecular biology, utilizing advanced high-resolution mass spectrometers coupled with robust chemometrics and bioinformatics pipelines.

My research focuses on developing innovative extraction, isolation, and chromatographic separation techniques for specialized metabolites and mammalian biomolecules. By integrating multi-omics data with structural spectrometry, I aim to decode complex regulatory pathways, understand stress adaptation mechanisms in high-altitude medicinal flora, and discover therapeutic leads.

Interdisciplinary Methodology

Our investigative workflows couple ion mobility spectrometry (IMS) with quadrupole time-of-flight (QTOF) mass spectrometry to resolve isobaric and isomeric secondary metabolites with high precision. Complementary pipelines combine transcriptomics and quantitative proteomics (DIA/DDA) to map environmental regulatory networks in medicinal species.

Beyond analytical characterization, my work extends to bioactivity validation—evaluating novel plant-derived peptides and natural products in disease models including cardioprotection, metabolic pathways, and antimicrobial action.

Scientific Domains

Core Research Interests

Four interconnected scientific domains guiding current investigations and future research initiatives.

Plant Metabolomics

Deciphering specialized secondary metabolism, constructing reference libraries, and resolving light-dependent regulatory mechanisms in medicinal flora.

Mass Spectrometry Imaging

Spatially resolving metabolites and proteins directly within tissue sections to track localization, biosites, and developmental transport dynamics.

Proteomics Systems

Employing DIA/DDA pipelines, medicinal proteomics, and intact protein/PTM profiling to uncover regulatory networks in disease and plant models.

Bioactive Peptide Discovery

Isolating, sequencing, and characterizing therapeutic peptides from natural sources with proven efficacy in cardiomyopathy and metabolic targets.

Empirical Discoveries

Selected Research Highlights

Authentic scientific achievements and methodological developments from ongoing doctoral investigations at CSIR-IHBT.

Metabolite Profiling & Library Development

Comprehensive Reference Map of 266 Specialized Compounds

Constructed a highly detailed metabolic reference map identifying 266 plant specialized compounds, establishing structural baselines and fragmentation libraries using high-resolution UHPLC-QTOF-MS/MS.

In Silico Drug Discovery & Pharmacology

Identification of 80 High-Potential Bioactive Leads

Conducted advanced molecular docking and network biology screening to identify 80 candidate bioactive leads for therapeutic application against key human physiological and metabolic targets.

Analytical Method Development & Validation

UHPLC-DAD-QTOF-IMS Method for Endangered Species

Developed and validated a rapid, robust analytical method for isolating and quantifying therapeutic diterpenoid alkaloids from the endangered medicinal plant Aconitum heterophyllum utilizing ion mobility spectrometry.

Multi-Omics Systems Biology

Photobiological Regulation of Secondary Metabolism

Leveraged transcriptomics and metabolomics in tandem to decipher the light-dependent transcriptional cascades and biochemical checkpoints that govern secondary metabolite biosynthesis under varying light regimes.
