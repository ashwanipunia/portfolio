Academic & Research Experience

Doctoral research appointments, specialized methodological investigations, and scientific deliverables conducted at the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research – Institute of Himalayan Bioresource Technology (CSIR-IHBT).

October 2026 – Till

Project Research Scientist 1

CSIR-Institute of Himalayan Bioresource Technology (CSIR-IHBT), Palampur, Himachal Pradesh, India

Project Title: PAN-India multicentric study on neurocpsticerosis: Unravelling its metabolomics and proteomics architecture to enables its diagnosis and understand epilepsy (CYST-OMICS)

Delving further into metabolomics, quantitative proteomics, and the search for bioactive chemicals. Seeking out secondary metabolite routes and unlocking medicinal potential by integrating bioinformatics algorithms with high-resolution mass spectrometry.

Core Scientific Contributions & Methodologies:

  • Multi-Omics Architecture & Biomarker Discovery: Spearhead metabolomics and proteomics discovery pipelines within the PAN-India multicentric CYST-OMICS consortium to identify and validate novel diagnostic biomarkers for neurocysticercosis (NCC).
  • Mechanistic Investigation: Characterize host-parasite metabolic and proteomic alterations across clinical cohorts to elucidate the molecular pathways driving NCC-induced epileptogenesis.
  • High-Throughput LC-MS Workflows: Direct end-to-end clinical sample preparation, quality control, and LC-MS analytical workflows for multi-site patient samples, ensuring high reproducibility and data integrity.
  • Cross-Institutional Collaboration: Coordinate sample logistics, standardized protocols, and multi-omics data integration across participating clinical and research centers at IIT-Mandi and CSIR-IHBT.
August 2020 – December 2025

PhD Researcher (Biological Sciences)

CSIR-Institute of Himalayan Bioresource Technology (CSIR-IHBT), Palampur, Himachal Pradesh, India

Degree-Granting Affiliation: Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR)

Conducted over five years of intensive doctoral research focused on metabolomics, quantitative proteomics, phytochemical characterization, and bioactive compound discovery. Integrated analytical mass spectrometry with bioinformatics pipelines to map secondary metabolite pathways and investigate their therapeutic properties.

Core Scientific Contributions & Methodologies:

  • Analytical Method Development for Endangered Species: Developed and validated a rapid, robust analytical methodology for the isolation, identification, and simultaneous quantification of eight therapeutic diterpenoid alkaloids from the critically endangered Himalayan medicinal plant Aconitum heterophyllum using UHPLC-DAD-QTOF-IMS (Ion Mobility Spectrometry).
  • Specialized Metabolite Reference Mapping: Constructed a comprehensive high-resolution metabolic reference map identifying 266 plant specialized compounds, generating authentic MS/MS fragmentation libraries and retention time/drift time profiles.
  • Targeted In Silico Lead Discovery: Conducted high-throughput molecular docking and network pharmacology analyses, discovering 80 high-potential bioactive leads for therapeutic applications against cardiovascular, antimicrobial, and metabolic targets.
  • Multi-Omics Photobiology Investigations: Leveraged coupled transcriptomic and metabolomic platforms to map light-dependent transcriptional cascades and enzymatic checkpoints regulating secondary metabolite biosynthesis under controlled photoperiods.
  • Cardioprotective Peptide Validation: Contributed to the multi-omics evaluation of a novel bioactive peptide (BP1), elucidating its protective mechanisms against isoproterenol-induced cardiomyopathy in zebrafish animal models through proteomic and histological assays.
  • Developmental Proteomics of Non-Model Plants: Generated proteomic and metabolomic datasets mapping seed germination and developmental stages in Ferula assa-foetida and dissected organ proteome maps in Picrorhiza kurroa.

Scholarly Dissemination

Conference Presentations & Symposiums

Invited research presentations, conference lectures, and poster symposiums presented at premier international and national scientific forums.

International Conference June 2025 ANRF ITS Grantee
Baltimore, Maryland, USA

Unraveling antioxidant and anti-inflammatory actions of Aconitum heterophyllum plant extract: Insights from metabolomics, molecular docking, and animal studies

73rd ASMS Conference on Mass Spectrometry and Allied Topics (ASMS 2025)

Characterized the tissue-specific metabolome of Aconitum heterophyllum (266 metabolites) and screened 10 bioactive candidates via molecular docking against antioxidant and immunomodulatory targets. Validated lead compounds (TC and CA) in vitro in RAW 264.7 macrophages, confirming potent ROS suppression, mitochondrial protection, and anti-inflammatory cytokine reduction (NO, IL-6, TNF-α, IL-1β) for pharmaceutical formulation.

National Symposium September 2024 CSIR Scientific Session
CSIR-IHBT, Palampur, India

Identification and quantitation of eight alkaloids in Aconitum heterophyllum using UHPLC-DAD-QTOF-IMS: A valuable tool for quality control

Seminar Series 2024

Presented a rapid, cost-effective analytical method with optimizing column, mobile phase, and run-time parameters to reduce solvent consumption, analysis time, and experimental costs. Established a scalable workflow for routine quality control (QC) and quantitative alkaloid profiling across diverse herbal formulations.

International Symposium March 2024 Best Poster Conferred
University of Hyderabad, India

Unique transcriptional landscape illustrates the diversified adaptation of medicinal plants to multifactorial stress combinations at high altitude

International Symposium on Plant Biology and Functional Genomics

Revealed molecular signatures linked with stress response and secondary metabolite buildup, hence supporting Picrorhiza kurroa adaptation, which is mostly controlled by a complex interaction of phytohormones, in a high-altitude alpine setting. The results identify a range of fresh candidate genes for resilience to climate change that would help to advance our studies on high-altitude alpine plant adaptations.
