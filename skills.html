High-Resolution Multi-Omics Workflow

Standardized five-phase analytical pipeline from botanical extraction to structural identification and bioinformatics integration.

PHASE 01
Extraction & Sample Prep

Lyophilization, ultrasonic/SPE extraction, protein precipitation, and internal standard spiking (accuracy > 98%).

PHASE 02
UHPLC Separation

Sub-2 μm reversed-phase (C18) & HILIC columns with dual DAD photodiode array detection and gradient optimization.

PHASE 03
Ion Mobility Spectrometry

Drift-tube IMS resolving isobaric and isomeric secondary metabolites via collision cross-section (CCS) measurements.

PHASE 04
QTOF MS/MS Fragmentation

High-resolution accurate mass (< 2 ppm) tandem MS/MS acquisition across low and high collision energies (CID).

PHASE 05
Bioinformatics & Modeling

MS-DIAL deconvolution, MetaboAnalyst pathway enrichment, molecular docking simulations, and network biology mapping.

Methodological Rigor & Quality Control

All analytical methods and mass spectrometry workflows adhere to international standards for precision, reproducibility, and mass accuracy (typically < 5 ppm). Routine calibration protocols, internal standards, and randomized run-order designs ensure data integrity across large-cohort metabolomics and quantitative proteomics studies.
